AI-assisted screening of foot photos

Three conditions we screen for

The detection model classifies every photo into one of these groups.

Normal

No sign of ulcer or gangrene. You get preventive foot-care advice.

Foot Ulcer (DFU)

An open sore, common in diabetes. Early medical care prevents infection and gangrene.

Gangrene

Dead tissue caused by lost blood supply or infection. It needs urgent medical attention.
